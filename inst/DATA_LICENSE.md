## Provenance qualification

The EdU fixtures have experimental lineage established by exact hash matches to
the documented publication archive. The protein-of-interest fixtures are
identified by the data owner as experimental Total MTBP data, but their exact
source derivation remains unverified. The related preprint association and data
license were supplied by the data owner, and the preprint license was verified
as CC BY-NC 4.0.

See `docs/REFERENCE_FIXTURE_PROVENANCE.md` and
`docs/reference-fixture-manifest.csv` in the source repository for file-level
hashes, provenance evidence, attestations, and unresolved fields.
